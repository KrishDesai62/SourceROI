It connects source versions, reviewed evaluation changes, task impact, evidence checks, diagnosis and auditable release decisions while preserving dataset evaluation, ablation and cost-aware selection.

Synthetic changes produce REVIEW when semantic review is missing and BLOCK for a protected regression. The original eight pillars remain operational in automated testing. Exact test counts and evidence are in PILOT_ACCEPTANCE_REPORT.md and the sprint summary.
