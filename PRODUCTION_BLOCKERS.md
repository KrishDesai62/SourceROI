No architectural framework was added to conceal these gaps. The CLI, dashboard, FastAPI and SQLite remain a restricted local beta. No SLA, uptime, SOC 2, regulatory, enterprise security or production-ready claim is made.

Priorities: (1) one genuine trusted local developer with authorized sources and reviewed labels; (2) verified actual application/provider execution accounting before inference; (3) full backup/restore rehearsal and durable job ownership; (4) parser isolation and dependency/security review; (5) authenticated access/tenancy only when a shared deployment is justified. The existing equal-input three-arm protocol remains the route to measuring differentiation, not a proven advantage.
