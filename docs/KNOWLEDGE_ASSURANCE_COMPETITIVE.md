# Competitive documentation audit

**Current grounding evidence:** strict gates prevent unreviewed grounded approval, and the main smoke produced retrieved citations, but final controls still failed abstention/format requirements. Maintained full-suite detection remains tied and human labor advantage unmeasured. [Grounding sprint](RELIABILITY_GROUNDING_SPRINT.md).

**Previous fair comparison:** with equivalent authoritative change information, SourceROI tied the maintained full-suite baseline on 13 exposed authored controls. Neither total maintenance savings nor a competitive advantage was established. [Fair comparison and limitations](VALIDATION_DIFFERENTIATION_SPRINT.md).

Verified November 13, 2026 from the primary documentation linked below. This is a public-documentation comparison, not a product trial, performance benchmark or exhaustive capability inventory. Absence from these pages is not evidence that a vendor lacks a feature. No commercial account or paid service was used.

**Industry-standard capabilities:** evaluation datasets and versions, generated tests, human review, lineage/dependency analysis, regression checks, controlled comparisons, reporting and cost accounting all overlap existing products and guidance.

**What SourceROI currently provides:** local immutable source/test snapshots; candidate passage/fact changes; hash-bound named review decisions; approved expectation overlays; annotation/history/topic dependency mapping; paired exact-answer/evidence checks; four-way release verdicts; diagnostic hypotheses; explicitly reviewed isolated repair; historical version reports; preserved source/document/passage ablations and optimizer.

**Potential workflow differentiator:** a developer can inspect a source update, decide what should change and what must remain correct, execute paired checks, and inspect supporting version/repair evidence in one compact local workflow. This is an integrated product hypothesis. It is not a claim that others lack it.

**Experimental support:** the latest fair controls supplied every approach the same approved answers, revised evidence and unresolved intent. SourceROI and the maintained full-suite answer-plus-evidence baseline both blocked 8/8 consequential cases, false-blocked 0/3 legitimate updates and returned REVIEW on two ambiguous cases. A stronger selector found the same failures but left three partial passing cases inconclusive. Three approved answer fields were prefilled, but five suggestions required review, including one incorrect and one ambiguous proposal. No human labor saving was measured. The earlier two-stale-label-false-alarm result used baselines without the updated labels and is not evidence of superiority over maintained alternatives. [Raw fair comparison](artifacts/validation-sprint-fair-comparison.json).


**Still unvalidated:** incremental benefit versus a well-maintained competitor or human-maintained regression workflow; actual review effort; customer failure rates; unseen selector recall; semantic/citation quality; deployment safety across arbitrary agents. No exclusivity, accuracy advantage, customer ROI or time-saved percentage is established.
